Expense Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Expense Tracker is an Android app for recording expenses, income and budgets. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in your own Google account. The app creates a Google Sheet called "Expense Tracker" and a Google Drive folder called "Expense Tracker Receipts". Your transactions, ledgers, categories and budgets are written to that sheet, and the bill photos and PDFs you attach are uploaded to that folder. The developer has no server and never receives a copy of your data.

Google permissions

The app asks for the drive.file permission only. With it, the app can see and edit only the files it creates (the sheet and receipts folder above). It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive. The app also reads your email address, only to show which account is connected.

Expense Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data on your phone

The app keeps a local copy of your sheet so it opens quickly and works offline. Entries made without internet are kept on the phone until they are uploaded. Bill photos are deleted from the phone once they are uploaded to your Drive. Text recognition (OCR) of bills runs entirely on the phone.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. There are no ads and no analytics.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove the local copy. Your sheet and receipts folder are ordinary files in your Google Drive; delete them there at any time. You can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
